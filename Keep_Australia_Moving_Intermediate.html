Teacher notes & sources

Designed for Year 9, within Victorian Curriculum F–10 Version 2.0 Economics and Business Levels 9–10. Learning focus: explain money flows, economic interdependence and trade-offs. This is a design alignment, not a quotation or certified curriculum mapping. Official curriculum wording could not be verified during creation because site access was denied; no curriculum codes are asserted.

Conceptual reference: RBA, Circular Flow Model, five-sector teaching model. Businesses and Banks are accessible classroom labels. Saving and lending arrows simplify a wider financial system; loans are not a one-for-one transfer of a particular household’s savings. The overseas arrows illustrate the direct-order and export cases used here.

References for teachers (internet required only to visit):

Use with KEEP AUSTRALIA MOVING — BANKSIA BAY CHALLENGE | YEAR 9 GAME COMPANION. Questions Q1–16 are mapped at each checkpoint. Recommended pacing: 3–4 minutes per round, 5–7 minutes for final discussion. No countdown, scores or penalty for retrying.

All dollar values are AUD and fictional teaching examples. Monthly loan quotes are simplified, not amortisation schedules. Trends describe the chosen case, not economic forecasts. No student data is persisted or transmitted. All interface assets are embedded.
